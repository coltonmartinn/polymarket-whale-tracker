A full scan of 200 markets typically takes 15–30 seconds. Every scan is saved, which is what powers the Momentum and Live Track Record tabs.

Whale momentum

Diffs the two most recent saved scans to show whales adding to, reducing, entering, or exiting near-certain positions. A scheduled task runs a scan every 30 minutes in the background, so this fills in even when the dashboard is closed.

Historical calibration backtest

When Polymarket prices something ≥95% or ≤5% in the final 15 days before it resolves, how often is it actually right? Sampled from already-resolved, binary, non-trivial-volume markets. This validates the core premise — separately from whale activity — before treating a "near-certain" price as meaningful.

Live track record

Every near-certain outcome this scanner has ever flagged is tracked until it resolves. This is the forward-looking version of the calibration backtest — built from our own calls, not history — and it starts empty. It grows as flagged markets resolve over the coming days and weeks.
